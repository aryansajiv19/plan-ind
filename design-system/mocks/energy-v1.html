So nothing below spends colour. §21 stays exactly as ratified. Every proposal here is scale, motion or density — three levers the app has never pulled, all of which work on text, and none of which need a photograph to arrive.

What I am actually proposing

One loud moment per screen, and the app currently has zero. The instinct that produced §20's one italic and §21's one fill is right and I am not reopening it — I am saying the budget was set to one and then never spent. Restraint against nothing reads as empty, which is exactly what the owner said twice.

  1. Scale, not colour, is the lever we have not pulled. Every change above is a font-size or a padding. Nothing here touches the palette, §21's placement rule, or the ceiling.
  2. The photo-less card should stop reserving space for a photo. Type fills it instead, using vibe, cuisine and open_till — fields that are on every row today and go unrendered. This is the change that touches 76 of 82 cards.
  3. Ungate the reveal from photography. It is the one genuinely joyful thing in the build and it has been invisible since it shipped.

What I did not do: put colour blocks back on cards, add a fourth instrument to §21, or propose anything that needs photography to arrive. If photography never scales past six, all three of these still work.
