The honest limitation, visible above. Compare the two grids. In light, a card carries two legible text colours plus a third for kickers: name in navy (18.10:1), meta in slate (12.02:1), kickers in brown (6.31:1). In dark, only one colour is legible on a card — grey drops to 4.24:1 and tan to 3.05:1 on #2D383E, both below the 4.5 floor, so name and meta are forced to the same value. Card interiors go mono-ink.

And the brown accent does not exist in dark at all — 2.87:1 on the canvas, 1.91:1 on cards. Dark's kickers above are tan, which works on the canvas (4.59:1) but would fail inside a card. So dark is a flatter system than light, not a re-skin. Fixing it means introducing a lighter grey the owner did not pick.
