as it ships today

This is the baseline, not the intent. It reproduces the shipped look — including the override blocks at globals.css:2936-2954 that turn off the signature token shadow and every hover transform. The richer design written above those blocks (parallax Dubai skyline, floating cards, a winner-stamp animation) never renders.

Colour

Ivory and graphite base, champagne for outcomes, one cobalt state accent, and five category-group hues. Every value is theme-scoped — day and night are separate systems, not a filter. Ratios measured against the darker ground in each theme.
