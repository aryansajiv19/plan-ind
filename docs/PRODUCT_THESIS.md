# What Planind is for

Owner, 2026-10-01: "If someone asks what I'm trying to solve, I can't just brag
about a flashy thing. It should genuinely solve something."

## The problem

A group of friends in Dubai cannot agree where to go. The chat runs for hours,
everyone has a different budget, distance and mood, and the plan dies ("we
should hang out" never happens).

## The one-line answer

Planind ends the "where should we go?" chat: one link, two minutes, one place
everyone is fine with, and it actually happens.

## Who it is for, and what each needs

| Person | Wants | So the product must |
|---|---|---|
| The host (the one who always organises) | To stop chasing people and arguing | Start in seconds (who's coming, what vibe), send one link, get one answer |
| The invited friend | To be asked as little as possible | Open the link and answer in ~10 seconds: no install, no account, a first name is enough |
| An investor | A frequent painful problem, a loop that grows itself, proof groups plan again, a believable way to earn | Each plan brings 3-5 new people through the link; measure repeat plans; venues or booking referrals pay |
| An engineer / CTO | A reliable, measurable core loop | Link opens fast, votes sync live, photos and costs do not depend on a lucky third-party call, a funnel with numbers |

## What the product really is

Not "vote 9 -> 3 -> 1". It is **finding the place that works for everyone**.
The argument in a chat is that everyone wants something different. The product
asks each friend three quick taps (budget comfort, how far, vibe or dealbreaker),
finds the overlap, and says why it fits: "under AED 150 for all of you, 15 minutes
from Marina for 4 of 5". A group chat cannot do that. The 3-round vote becomes an
optional tie-breaker for big groups.

## The loop to protect

link -> a friend answers in 10 seconds -> a decision -> a plan that happens
(time, who books, calendar) -> they host the next one.

## What to measure (needs analytics; none exists yet)

- Median time from "plan started" to "decided" (target: under 5 minutes).
- Share of invited friends who respond without an account (target: 70%+).
- Share of decided plans that actually happen (ask after).
- Groups that plan a second time within 30 days.

## Status (2026-10-01)

- Built: the decision step (compose, deal, vote, decided), live votes, a drawn map.
- Unproven: nobody has run a real group through it. The next milestone is 10 real
  groups, not more screens.
- Not built: group preferences, guest voting (staged on lane/guest-vote), analytics.
- Decision for the owner: make "group preferences" the next big build.
